# Northwind Robotics — Product Line

Northwind Robotics sells three home robots:
- NW-Vac One — robotic vacuum, 120-minute battery, lidar navigation, $499.
- NW-Mop Duo — combined vacuum and mop, 90-minute battery, $649.
- NW-Lawn Pro — robotic lawn mower, covers up to 0.5 acre, $1,299.

All products ship with a 1-year limited warranty and a 30-day return window.
