# Vertical farming

Vertical farming grows crops in stacked layers indoors under controlled light,
temperature, and humidity. It trades higher energy use for much lower land and
water use.

- Water use can be 90 to 95 percent lower than open-field farming because water
  is recirculated.
- Crops grow year round, independent of weather and season.
- Leafy greens and herbs are the most common crops because they grow fast and
  are light.
- LED lighting is the single largest operating cost.
- Yields per square meter are far higher than open fields because of stacking.
- Proximity to cities shortens supply chains and cuts transport spoilage.
